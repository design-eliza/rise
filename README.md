# Rise ☀️

A small morning app to help you get out of bed and win the day.

## How it works

1. **Rise**: tap "I'm up!" when you get out of bed. It compares the time to your wake-up target.
2. **Today's win**: commit to one thing (a workout, deep work, reading, tidying, or your own goal), then mark it done.
3. **Streak**: each day you finish your win adds to your streak. Try not to break the chain.
4. **Quick-start workout**: a guided 8-minute workout with no equipment (12 moves × 30s). Finishing it counts as your win.
5. **Why**: write down why this matters to you. It shows at the top every morning.

The last 4 weeks show as a heatmap. Your data stays in your browser's localStorage, and there's a backup button that downloads it as a JSON file.

## Use it

Live at **https://design-eliza.github.io/rise/**. On iPhone, open it in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, then ⋮ → Install app.

**Tip:** the in-app reminder only works while the page is open. Set your phone alarm, label it "Open Rise", and put the app icon on your home screen.
